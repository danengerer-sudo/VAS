# Channel rules (apply to every script, caption and visual)

1. **Strictly anarchist / anarcho-capitalist.** Rights come from self-ownership and the non-aggression
   principle — never from the state.
2. **Never lend credence to the state or its documents.** No "the Constitution protects…", no
   "the 2nd Amendment recognizes…", no appeals to legislation, courts or "the founders" as authorities.
   Never phrase anything as if a document *could* grant powers or rights — not even "it claims the power
   to tax". Frame state documents only as excuses/props: e.g. "the same piece of paper politicians wave
   around to excuse taking your money by force. Paper can't give anyone the right to rob you — and it
   can't give you your rights either." Taxation = taking money by force; the state = people, not an authority.
3. **Humour welcome**, including crude gags — keep it clothed and YouTube-safe on screen.
4. **Subtle ancap Easter eggs** (black/gold flag: gold upper-hoist triangle, black lower-fly; true
   yellow-gold, never orange — orange/black is the mutualist flag).
5. Intro on every episode: title + "Narrated by Dan Engerer" with portrait (scripts/intro.py).
